Status means one clear thing.

PingStep reports what it has received. It does not guess whether a silent job is healthy.

Running

Recent updates are arriving. The last reported stage is shown.

Stale

Updates stopped past the time you set. The job may still be running, but PingStep cannot confirm it.

Succeeded

Your script explicitly sent a successful completion event.

Failed

Your script explicitly sent a failed completion event. It is never shown as complete.

Only the signal you choose.

PingStep stores your account email, job names, lifecycle events, timestamps, and token hashes so it can show a run’s status.

Never your job’s contents.

It does not collect raw logs, command output, SQL, credentials, or the files your job processes. Read the privacy details.
