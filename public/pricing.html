How payment works

Choose a plan, complete secure Stripe Checkout, and PingStep automatically updates access after Stripe confirms the subscription. PingStep never receives card details.

Changing plans

Use Manage billing to switch between Pro and Team. Upgrades take effect immediately and Stripe charges the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect immediately; unused paid value becomes a Stripe credit toward future invoices.

Self-service refunds

PingStep does not provide manual plan changes or cash refunds for plan changes. Manage subscriptions and billing details in Stripe. This policy does not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

Why limits exist

Lifecycle events use real compute and database capacity. Limits keep the free trial useful while protecting all customers from noisy or accidental high-frequency integrations.
